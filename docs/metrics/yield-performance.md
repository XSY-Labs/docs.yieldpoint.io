# Yield Performance

## Important Caveats on Reported Returns

> ⚠️ **"Since launch" figures (e.g. 10.41%, 16.7%) referenced in product briefs use a June 2025 inception date, which corresponds to the v1 XSY deployment.** These figures span both the v1 (XSY, deprecated) and v2 (YieldPoint, current) deployments and **cannot be reconstructed from the v2 on-chain indexer alone**.

> ⚠️ **Annualized v2-only share price growth from the v2 inception date of 2026-03-26 is approximately ~78% as of May 2026.** This figure is dominated by early-stage donation seeding activity and is **not representative of steady-state yield**. It should not be presented to users as an expected ongoing return.
