# yUTY APY Methodology

> **Important:** yUTY exposes two distinct APY figures that are frequently confused in marketing materials. They are **not** interchangeable and must **not** be averaged together.

---

## 1. Strategy APY

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Definition** | Raw trade engine return on Trade NAV, computed off-chain from the basis trade PnL ledger |
| **What it measures** | Delta-neutral hedge performance **before** insurance fund retention |
| **Reference period** | June 2025 – February 2026 |
| **Historical value** | **16.7 %** |

Strategy APY represents the gross yield generated by the underlying basis trade. It is calculated off-chain and reflects total PnL before any portion is retained by the insurance fund.

---

## 2. Distributed APY

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Definition** | Share price growth observable on-chain via `convertToAssets()` or the `stg__donation.new_share_price` progression |
| **What it measures** | Yield **actually received** by yUTY holders |
| **As of** | 2026-05-18 |
| **Current value** | **9 %** |

Distributed APY is the yield that accrues directly to yUTY vault share holders. It is fully verifiable on-chain.

---

## 3. Why the Gap Exists

Distributed APY is structurally lower than Strategy APY because **not all UTY is staked into yUTY**. Some holders opt for points via **xUTY** or hold raw UTY for collateral utility. Retained PnL on unstaked UTY accrues to the **insurance fund**, which expands the maximum safely scalable TVL.

This two-token design is **intentional, not a leak**.

```
Strategy APY (16.7 %)  =  Distributed APY (9 %)  +  Insurance Fund Retention
```

---

## 4. Common Confusion & Correct Usage

- Marketing materials sometimes quote **16 % APY** (Strategy APY) and sometimes **9–10 % APY** (Distributed APY).
- These are **NOT** the same number.
- They should **never** be averaged or used interchangeably.
- When communicating yield to yUTY holders, always use the **Distributed APY** (9 %).
- When discussing strategy-level performance, always clearly label it as **Strategy APY** (16.7 %) and note it is before insurance fund retention.

---

## 5. On-Chain Verification

Distributed APY can be independently verified by querying:

```solidity
yUTY.convertToAssets(1e18) // returns current share price in UTY
```

or by inspecting the `stg__donation.new_share_price` event progression in the vault contract's event log.
