# yUTY Product Brief

> **Last updated: May 2026**

## Target APY

The current target APY for yUTY is **10%**.

This represents a downward revision from the **13%** target published in the Q2 and Q3 2025 product briefs. The revision reflects the maturation of the underlying strategy and updated risk-adjusted return expectations.

| Period | Target APY |
|---|---|
| Q2 2025 | 13% |
| Q3 2025 | 13% |
| May 2026 (current) | **10%** |

### Observed Band

The current observed APY band is **8–12%**, with the live trailing 30-day realized APY at approximately **9%**. See the `yUTY.trailing_30d_apy` claim for the latest realized figure.

> ⚠️ **Note:** Any references to a 12–15% target band in `protocol/architecture.md` or other legacy documents are superseded by this brief.
